The Story

The retro robot spirit returns from deep space.

Born from a signal travelling across deep space, D.K. FLEED carries the spirit of an era when giant mechanical heroes defended distant worlds and every new adventure began among the stars. It is a tribute to the imagination, courage and unforgettable atmosphere of classic 1970s and 1980s robot animation—reborn as an original, community-driven meme for a new digital generation.

Remember the thrill of an early weekend morning? Steel giants rising from secret hangars, comets streaking across hand-painted skies, heroic theme songs and cliffhangers that made the wait for the next episode feel endless. Those worlds taught a whole generation about courage, friendship and never giving up.

D.K. FLEED picks up that old signal and brings it to Solana as an independent meme project. No corporate backers, no hidden insiders, no complicated promises—just a crew of dreamers, collectors and space-adventure fans who never stopped looking up at the stars.

Yesterday’s cosmic dreams. Today’s meme community.

Protect yourself

Safety

Scammers love new projects. Read this before interacting with anything that uses the D.K. FLEED name.

  • There is no presale and no private sale. Anyone offering one is a scammer.
  • The team will never DM you first and will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys.
  • This website has no wallet connect, no transactions and no blockchain functions.
  • Only trust the contract address published on this website and official channels.
  • Do your own research and never risk more than you can afford to lose.
